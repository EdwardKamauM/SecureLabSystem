Getting Started

Creating Your Account
  1. Visit the registration page by clicking Register on the login screen.
  2. Fill out the registration form with your personal details.
  3. Select your appropriate role (Student, Faculty, or Technician).
  4. Create a strong password following the security requirements.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA verification.
  6. Submit your registration and wait for admin approval.
Note: Your account must be approved by an administrator before you can log in. This usually takes 1-2 business days.

Navigating the Dashboard

  1. After logging in, you'll be directed to your role-specific dashboard.
  2. The top section shows overview statistics relevant to your role.
  3. The Quick Actions section provides shortcuts to common tasks.
  4. Recent bookings or activities are displayed at the bottom.
  5. Use the navigation bar at the top to access other sections.
Tip: The dashboard is personalized based on your role, showing only the information and actions relevant to you.
